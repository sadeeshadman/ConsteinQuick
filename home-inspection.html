Home Inspection

Clear, honest, and thorough inspections so you can make confident decisions about your property.

What's included

Inspection services for every stage

Whether you're buying, selling, or building, our inspectors give you the full picture with detailed reports and straightforward advice.

  • Pre-Purchase Home Inspection

    A full evaluation of the property's structure, systems, and safety before you finalize your purchase.

  • Pre-Listing Inspection

    Identify issues before you list, so you can price accurately and avoid surprises during negotiations.

  • New Construction Inspection

    An independent review of newly built homes to catch deficiencies before your builder's warranty period ends.

  • Condo Inspection

    Focused inspections covering the unit and shared systems relevant to condominium ownership.

  • Thermal Imaging Inspection

    Infrared imaging to detect hidden moisture, insulation gaps, and electrical hot spots.

  • WETT Inspection

    Wood Energy Technology Transfer inspections for fireplaces, wood stoves, and chimneys — often required by insurers.
