About Constein Group

An Ottawa-based team built around one idea: property work should be handled honestly, carefully, and on time.

Our Story

Local expertise across property management, inspection, and construction

Constein Group was founded to give Ottawa property owners a single, dependable partner for the services their properties need most — managing tenants, inspecting homes, and building or renovating spaces.

Instead of juggling multiple contractors and companies, our clients work with one team that understands the full picture of their property, from tenant relationships to structural condition to construction quality.

We're proud to serve homeowners, landlords, buyers, sellers, and investors throughout Ottawa and the surrounding communities.

What We Value

The principles behind every project

  • Honest, straightforward communication at every step
  • Careful attention to detail on every report and job site
  • Transparent, upfront pricing with no surprise fees
  • Respect for your time, your property, and your budget
  • Accountability — we stand behind our work
