Complete property management services

From finding the right tenant to keeping your property in great shape, Constein Group manages every aspect of your rental so you can enjoy the return without the hassle.

  • Tenant Screening & Placement

    Thorough background, credit, and reference checks to place reliable tenants quickly and reduce turnover.

  • Rent Collection & Financial Reporting

    On-time rent collection with clear monthly statements, so you always know exactly where your property stands.

  • Property Maintenance & Repairs

    A trusted network of contractors handles routine maintenance and repairs quickly and at fair prices.

    Drafting, renewing, and enforcing leases in line with Ontario landlord-tenant regulations.

  • Move-In / Move-Out Inspections

    Documented condition reports that protect your property and simplify deposit reconciliation.

  • 24/7 Emergency Response

    Round-the-clock support for urgent maintenance issues, giving tenants and owners peace of mind.
